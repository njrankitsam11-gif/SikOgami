Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

SikOgami stores minimal data. Auth: We store your name, email (lowercased, unique), and a bcrypt-hashed password in Neon Postgres sikogami_users (no plain text). Progress: Level IDs you complete are stored in sikogami_progress linked to your user id, so you can resume on any device. Scan images: When you tap Scan, we send a base64 image to Gemini Vision for verification; we do not persist images unless you opt in — preview stays on-device, and we show “On-device preview only”.

Cookies / localStorage: We use localStorage keys sikogami_users, sikogami_currentUser, sikogami_progress, sikogami_theme for offline fallback and theme. No third-party tracking cookies.

Sharing: We do not sell data. Affiliate paper links are plain links; no PII sent. Contact email hello@sikogami.vercel.app is only used to reply.

Rights: Email us to delete your account and all rows. Deleting cascades to progress via FK.

Retention: Neon branch production retains history 6 hours (Neon default). Backups follow Neon/Vercel defaults.
