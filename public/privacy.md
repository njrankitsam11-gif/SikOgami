# Privacy Policy — SikOgami

**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

SikOgami stores minimal data. **Auth:** name, email (lowercased, unique), and bcrypt-hashed password in `sikogami_users`. **Progress:** Level IDs in `sikogami_progress`. **Scan images:** base64 sent to Gemini Vision for verification; not persisted unless you opt in.

**Cookies / localStorage:** `sikogami_users`, `sikogami_currentUser`, `sikogami_progress`, `sikogami_theme` for offline fallback and theme. No third-party tracking.

**Sharing:** No data sold. Affiliate links are plain.

**Rights:** Email `hello@sikogami.vercel.app` to delete account (cascades to progress).
