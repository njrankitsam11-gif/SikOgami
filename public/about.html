About SikOgami

SikOgami (from Sik + Kami — to fold paper) is a zen gamified origami platform. Fold real paper at your desk, scan it with your phone via Gemini Vision, and level up from Paper Boat (Level 1, one sheet, 12 micro-steps) to SikOgami Castle (Level 30, twelve sheets). No timers, no fail penalty — just you, paper, and breath.

We built SikOgami because the modern web is stressful. Origami is proven to improve mindfulness, fine-motor control, spatial reasoning and patience. Teachers use it for geometry, parents for bonding, artists for flow. Our CraftPlayer shows every fold as a clean SVG with red valley / blue mountain dotted lines, turn-over hints, and a morphing preview that actually becomes the final object — not a static diagram.

The journey is 5 worlds: World 1 The Calm Shore (1-6, 1 paper, beginner: Boat, Cup, Tulip, Whale, Dog, Heart), World 2 The Quiet Forest (7-12, 1 paper, easy-medium: Butterfly, Fox, Frog, Crane, Fish, Penguin), World 3 The Bloom River (13-18, 2 papers: Tulip+Stem, Boat+Sail, etc), World 4 The Shadow Temple (19-24, 2-6 modular: Cube, Ninja Star), World 5 The Master's Peak (25-30, master: Dragon, Castle 12 papers). Each level lists paper type (Kami, duo, washi), size 15cm, and technique notes.

We monetize lightly: curated paper packs (Toyo Kami, Oddy Washi, Daler-Rowney) via affiliate 8–12% and brand collabs. Any paper works — even newspaper — but good paper folds better. Use code SIK15 for 15% off.

Stack: vanilla JS SPA + Tailwind CDN, Neon Postgres (US East 2) for auth & progress sync, Vercel serverless, Gemini 1.5 Flash for scan verification (with mock fallback). Dark/light theme, Zen Mode breathing (4-6, 4-4, 4-7-8), and a Zen Garden collection that syncs across devices via /api/progress.
