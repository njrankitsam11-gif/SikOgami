# About SikOgami

SikOgami (from *Sik + Kami* — to fold paper) is a zen gamified origami platform. Fold real paper at your desk, scan it with your phone via Gemini Vision, and level up from Paper Boat (Level 1, one sheet, 12 micro-steps) to SikOgami Castle (Level 30, twelve sheets). No timers, no fail penalty — just you, paper, and breath.

We built SikOgami because the modern web is stressful. Origami is proven to improve mindfulness, fine-motor control, spatial reasoning and patience.

## The Journey — 5 Worlds

- **World 1 The Calm Shore** (1-6, 1 paper, beginner: Boat, Cup, Tulip, Whale, Dog, Heart)
- **World 2 The Quiet Forest** (7-12, 1 paper, easy-medium: Butterfly, Fox, Frog, Crane, Fish, Penguin)
- **World 3 The Bloom River** (13-18, 2 papers: Tulip+Stem, Boat+Sail, etc)
- **World 4 The Shadow Temple** (19-24, 2-6 modular: Cube, Ninja Star)
- **World 5 The Master's Peak** (25-30, master: Dragon, Castle 12 papers)

Each level lists paper type (Kami, duo, washi), size 15cm, and technique notes.

## Stack

Vanilla JS SPA + Tailwind CDN, Neon Postgres (US East 2) for auth & progress sync, Vercel serverless, Gemini 1.5 Flash for scan verification.
